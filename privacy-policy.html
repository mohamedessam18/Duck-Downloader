Privacy Policy for Duck Downloader

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Duck Downloader ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application, Duck Downloader (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data directly from our users. All downloads and files are stored locally on your device or in your secure local vault.

However, the App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services: Used to run the application on Android devices.
  • Google AdMob: Used to serve advertisements in the free version of the App. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and ad-related data to show relevant ads.

2. App Access and Permissions

The App requests access to your device storage in order to download and save media files. This permission is used solely for functionality and no media files are uploaded to our servers.

3. Security

The App provides a secure private vault to store your downloaded media. The files in the vault are obfuscated and stored locally on your device, and are not accessible by third parties or sent to any servers.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
